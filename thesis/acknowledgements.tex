My supervisor, Dr.\ Tingting Mu, asked the question that changed the shape of this
project: what happens after the negotiation ends, and who explains it to the person
who has to live with the outcome? The Summary agent exists because of that
question, and the evaluation chapter is more honest for it.

Fusion21 hosted this work as an industrial partner and supplied the four internal
documents that the agent designs are specified against. Phil, my contact there,
reframed the project in a single sentence early on --- the interesting problem is
stopping disputes, not replaying them --- and the pre-award risk screen in
Section~\ref{sec:risk-screen} is the direct result. I am grateful to the Fusion21
staff who sat through a board demonstration and then handed me two of their own
documents to run through the system, which promptly found two defects that months
of synthetic testing had not.

The GPU work was carried out on the University of Manchester's Ronin research
computing service, without which the corpus expansion described in
Section~\ref{sec:corpus} would not have been affordable in the time available.
