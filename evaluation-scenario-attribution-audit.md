# Scenario-attribution audit — 5 Sep 2026

One-off analysis, not a permanent script (see RESULTS-FOR-THESIS.md's "post-Phase-5" section for why this
was run and where it feeds into the thesis). Raw hit data generated by a throwaway script, not committed;
this file is the durable record of what was found and how each hit was judged.

## Method

Searched every `compliance_checks[].reasoning` across all 406 negotiation logs (root `negotiation_log_*.json`
+ every `batch_results/batch_*/run_*.json`) for sentences matching a deliberately wide regex over
self-referential claims about the scenario's own content:

```
(the )?scenario( description)?( itself)?\s+(states|say[s]?|indicates|tells us|shows|confirms|describes|clarifies|specifies)
| as (stated|given|described|indicated) in the scenario
| the facts as given state
| scenario (states|says|confirms|indicates) that
```

For every hit, the claimed content was checked against that run's own embedded `scenario.description` —
the same field, read directly from the log, that `analyze_outcome_leakage.py`'s pooling fix already
established as the authoritative record of what that specific run was actually given.

## Result

**23 hits across 406 logs.** Deduplicated to 7 unique scenario descriptions referenced. **4 of 23 (17.4%)
are confirmed false** — the claimed content does not appear in that run's own embedded scenario. **Only 1
of the 4 is factually backwards from the real disposition specifically**; the other 3 are fabricated
subsidiary/evidentiary details that don't themselves assert who won.

## Conclusions this analysis supports

- **The pattern is real but rare**: 4 confirmed false claims out of 23 candidate sentences, out of ~1,500+
  compliance-check rounds screened across the corpus (406 logs, most with 2-5 rounds each).
- **Not confined to leak-free re-runs or to one court prompt version**: false instances appear in an old
  leaked-era batch (Parkingeye, pre-Phase-3), a leak-free re-run (Woods), a V3 batch (Alstom), and a V4
  batch (Alstom) — spanning both prompt versions and both eras.
- **Only 1 of 4 is disposition-backwards** (Woods) — the other 3 fabricate subsidiary facts (a notice
  detail, a score value, a sub-criteria dataset) in service of reasoning, not the ultimate finding itself.
- **This search caught a real fabrication instance the number-screen missed** (`run_17`'s invented
  sub-scores) — raising the corpus-wide confirmed Court-originated fabrication count from 12 to **13**.
